assets. The packaged PNG files under `draft2/` are byte-for-byte copies of the
sealed sources; no result was recomputed or redrawn.

1. `Figure 1`: `fig1_pythia160.png` from ARC-003,
   `fig1_pythia70.png` from ARC-002, and `fig1_hellaswag.png` from ARC-011.
2. `Figure 2`: `fig2_magnitude_matched.png` and
   `fig2_damage_matched.png` from ARC-004.
3. `Figure 3`: `fig3_match_coverage.png` from ARC-006's
   outcome-independent matching diagnostic and `fig3_corrected_residual.png`
   from corrected ARC-006R. The invalid ARC-006 outcome is not used.
4. `Figure 4`: `fig4_residual_boundary.png` and
   `fig4_layer_adjustment.png` from ARC-007R.

Draft 1's manuscript-native conceptual Figure 5 was removed as redundant
during page-budget compression. The empirical Figures 1--4 are unchanged.
