- Additional Pythia seeds after the existing 9+5 runs.
- Finer checkpoint trajectories without claiming monotonicity.

## DO NOT RUN

- Another alpha-calibration or internal-direction identification ARC under the current assay.
- Unpreregistered representation-metric fishing.
- A 1B+ sweep before corpus and construct validity are settled.
- Decorative seed multiplication that cannot change the decision.
- Large benchmark sweeps or new mechanism branches before the paper thesis survives novelty review.
- Any experiment designed only to recover the invalidated ARC-006 estimate.
