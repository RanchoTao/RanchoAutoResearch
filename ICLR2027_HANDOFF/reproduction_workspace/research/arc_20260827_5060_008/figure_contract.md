# Figure contract

Up to five reproducible static PNGs:

1. cell-level cross-family hidden-direction cosine distribution, faceted by run;
2. corrected family residual versus internal directional disagreement;
3. paired block-direction versus noise-direction `D_S` on eligible cells, with
   run summaries and the zero/equivalence reference;
4. counterfactual direction effect by adequately supported layer;
5. reversal versus non-reversal hidden disagreement and norm ratio.

White background, quiet gray guides, dark ink, blue/orange/gold only; use open
markers or line style in addition to color. Show cell grain and support in
titles/subtitles. Research blossom stays top-right. Omit unsupported figures
rather than decorating insufficient data; inspect every exported image.
