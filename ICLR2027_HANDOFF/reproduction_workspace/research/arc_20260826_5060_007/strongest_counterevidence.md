# Strongest counterevidence

The central counterevidence is upstream of the geometry hypothesis: ARC-006's
family residual is not a like-for-like outcome contrast. A single Class A
cell's residual changes by as much as 0.0180845 when the top-1 rule is made
consistent, compared with a frozen aggregate magnitude of 0.0192347.

The negative aggregate direction surviving both consistent rules is not enough
to waive this defect because ARC-007 targets cell-, layer-, and sign-specific
residual structure. Those are precisely the quantities most vulnerable to a
cell-dependent correction.
