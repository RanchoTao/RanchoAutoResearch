## Citation policy for the manuscript

- Cite Garcia and Lad et al. in the Introduction when stating the problem.
- Cite DTM at the first mathematical definition of `S`.
- Describe SteerCheck, Reblitz-Richardson, Kim et al., Walking Noise, and APEX
  according to their actual intervention regimes; do not imply direct
  replication.
- Do not call any arXiv-only work peer reviewed.
