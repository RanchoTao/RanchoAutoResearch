# Figure contract

Static PNG is the selected surface because ARC-006R is an offline local
research artifact.

1. **Corrected aggregate residual:** horizontal point-and-interval comparison
   of invalid mixed ARC-006, canonical lowest-index, and consistent-`topk`.
   Show the zero line and frozen equivalence band. Hard two-root palette.
2. **Layer repair comparison:** paired old-versus-canonical layer means with
   exact layer counts and low-support warning. Use marker shape/fill as well as
   color so the comparison remains readable without color.

Titles remain descriptive. Subtitles state Class A, run-cluster uncertainty,
and the frozen population. Figures are generated from `corrected_results.csv`
and inspected at exported resolution.
