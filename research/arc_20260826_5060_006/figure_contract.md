# Figure contract

Surface: six regenerable static Matplotlib PNG files requested by the ARC.
Final QA is visual inspection at original resolution.

1. Target versus achieved KL and NLL: two-panel scatter with identity lines;
   target-cell grain and match-class marker shapes.
2. ARC-005 versus ARC-006 coverage: two-category bar with exact counts/rates;
   zero baseline.
3. Prospectively matched `D_S`: per-run paired small multiples.
4. Family residual: run-level dot/interval forest with frozen equivalence band.
5. Residual shrinkage: two-estimate dot/interval comparison, not an
   underpowered time-series line.
6. Strongest counterevidence: the prespecified worst seed/checkpoint stratum or
   a target-accuracy plot if matching failure is the decisive evidence.

Palette policy: hard two-root cap, blue filled circles for block deletion,
orange open triangles for activation noise, gold equivalence/reference fill,
and charcoal axes/identity lines. Color is always paired with marker/fill or
faceting. Titles are neutral; subtitles state grain, n, and frozen thresholds.
Research blossom is fixed at the top-right. No smoothing hides cell-level
heterogeneity.
