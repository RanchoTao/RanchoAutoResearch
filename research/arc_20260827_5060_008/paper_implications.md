# Paper implications

## Strongest safe claim

At the frozen block-output site, the learned block-deletion update and existing
Gaussian-noise intervention are nearly orthogonal across all five runs. Hidden
norm ratio carries modest observational information about residual/layer
heterogeneity, while angular disagreement adds little because it scarcely
varies. The present assay cannot independently manipulate direction and
downstream damage with adequate support.

## Overclaims

- Internal perturbation direction causes Candidate A.
- Direction swapping changes corrected `D_S`.
- Direction explains the 24 sign reversals.
- Direction explains layer heterogeneity.
- The failed calibration is evidence of no direction effect.

The result does not kill the direction mechanism; it kills this causal
identification design. A paper cannot use the 46.4% observational layer
reduction as mechanism evidence without a balanced intervention.
