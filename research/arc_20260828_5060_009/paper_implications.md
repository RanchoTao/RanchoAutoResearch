# Paper implications

The safe result is an assay limitation: under the frozen internal-direction
counterfactual, scalar alpha can usually match KL/NLL damage, but it cannot
simultaneously preserve the required hidden/output perturbation state across a
broad majority of cells. This prevents a well-powered causal direction claim.

It would be overstated to say that internal direction is irrelevant, or that a
direction swap has no effect. The outcome was never accessed. Candidate A's
validated empirical phenomenon and prior mechanism falsifications remain
separate evidence; ARC-009 only closes this identification branch.
