# Figure contract

Static PNG is the required report surface. At most four figures will be made:

1. categorical count bar for PASS-A, PASS-B, and failure classes;
2. distribution panels for selected alpha multiplier and frozen target/pair
   mismatch, with all 103 cells retained;
3. count heatmaps/bars for seed, checkpoint, and layer PASS-A coverage;
4. horizontal count bar for failure modes.

Charts use an explicit blue/orange/neutral palette, direct counts, zero-based
count axes, and non-color labels. No outcome metric is plotted. Every exported
PNG will be visually inspected at full resolution.
