# Figure contract

Surface: reproducible static Matplotlib PNG files, visually inspected at final
resolution. White background, charcoal text, quiet gray grid, blue/orange hard
two-root palette, non-color markers/line styles, and research blossom fixed at
top right. Titles are descriptive and subtitles state Class A/sample grain.

1. Paired family geometry: three faceted paired-dot comparisons for mean
   `delta_b` (equal to `delta_margin`), absolute cosine, and flip rate.
2. Relationship scatter: 103 Class A cells, x = noise-minus-block mean
   `delta_b`, y = frozen family residual, with run marker/facet context.
3. Uncertainty/benchmark forest: M0-M3 family coefficients and cluster-
   bootstrap intervals against the frozen equivalence band.
4. Margin strata: run-level interval estimates for low/medium/high intact
   margin, displayed as discrete dots rather than a spurious trend line.
5. Layer comparison: raw and geometry-adjusted family effects by layer, with
   low-support layers visibly open-marked.
6. Sign reversal: standardized geometry differences for residual-negative
   versus positive-reversal cells, with exact group sizes.
7. Strongest counterevidence: per-run M0 versus M2 coefficients, emphasizing
   the run with least shrinkage or greatest movement away from zero.

Axes include zero and equivalence references whenever scientifically relevant.
No smoothing is used to hide heterogeneity.
