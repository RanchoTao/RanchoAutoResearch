# Layer analysis

Family coefficients were estimated within each corrected layer using the same
damage-only and M3 geometry specifications. Layer 7 has only three cells from
two runs and remains explicitly low support.

| Layer | Cells | Runs | Damage-only | Full geometry |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 15 | 5 | 0.00367 | 0.01871 |
| 2 | 15 | 5 | -0.00837 | -0.00441 |
| 3 | 10 | 4 | -0.02611 | -0.02325 |
| 4 | 14 | 5 | -0.03788 | -0.03185 |
| 5 | 9 | 4 | -0.02687 | -0.03998 |
| 6 | 8 | 5 | -0.03403 | -0.04903 |
| 7 | 3 | 2 | -0.04407 | -0.05441 (low support) |
| 8 | 8 | 3 | -0.01566 | -0.01306 |
| 9 | 12 | 5 | -0.02251 | -0.01718 |
| 10 | 9 | 4 | 0.01774 | 0.01188 |

The cell-weighted layer SD increased from `0.01737` to `0.02192`, a shrinkage
of `-26.18%`. Geometry therefore does not explain layer heterogeneity; the
adjustment makes it larger. Among adequately supported layers, layer 6 retains
the largest negative adjusted effect (`-0.04903`). No claim rests on layer 7.
