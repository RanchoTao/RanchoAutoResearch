# Prohibited claims

The following statements, or paraphrases that imply them, are excluded from
Draft 0 and later revisions unless new evidence is explicitly authorized and
obtained.

- “We discover that layer equivalence changes during training.”
- “We are the first to show intervention-protocol dependence.”
- “`S` or `Delta S` is a novel metric.”
- “KL or NLL causes `Delta S`.”
- “Functional predictive damage fully explains the response.”
- “Intervention family is irrelevant after damage matching.”
- “The result is universal across language models.”
- “The effect scales to large language models.”
- “Internal perturbation direction causes the residual.”
- “We identified the causal mechanism.”
- “Raw perturbation magnitude is irrelevant to language-model perturbations in general.”
- “The trajectory is strictly monotone or constitutes a universal training law.”
- “The assay establishes downstream capability loss, deployment fragility, or pruning safety.”
- “Two corpora establish domain, language, or dataset universality.”

## Rewrite rule

Any sentence that attributes a protocol-conditioned measurement directly to a
protocol-independent layer property must be rewritten to name the intervention,
model family, scale, corpus, and measured endpoint that support it.
