# Exact novelty delta

## What the closest work already tells us

Existing work establishes that layer-redundancy/equivalence measurements depend
on intervention protocol, that related measurements evolve across individual
pretraining trajectories, and that top-1 disagreement and KL-based functional
budgets are established analysis tools.

## What Candidate A adds

Candidate A tests whether the observed protocol dependence can be reduced to
differences in functional predictive damage. Across independent small-Pythia
pretraining runs, it prospectively matches block bypass and norm-controlled
activation noise on joint KL/NLL damage. A corrected deterministic top-1 assay
retains a seed-stable family-specific residual, and the underlying block-bypass
endpoint direction repeats on two evaluation corpora.

## One-sentence answer

After this paper, a reader has controlled evidence that, in the tested
small-Pythia setting, matching two intervention families on output-level KL/NLL
damage does not make their top-1 training responses interchangeable, and that
this conclusion rests on independent-run and dual-corpus replication rather
than a single released trajectory.

This statement does not claim novelty for the metric, the broad training
phenomenon, matched-KL auditing in general, or a causal mechanism.
