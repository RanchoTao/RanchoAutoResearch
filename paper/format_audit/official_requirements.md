# Official local requirements

This summary is derived only from the locally supplied
`iclr2027_conference.tex` and `iclr2027_conference.sty`.

- Use LaTeX2e with `iclr2027_conference.sty` and
  `iclr2027_conference.bst`.
- Initial submissions are anonymous. `\iclrfinalcopy` must remain disabled;
  the official style then prints `Anonymous authors` and the under-review
  header with line numbers.
- Non-anonymous submissions are rejected without review.
- The initial-submission main-text limit is 9 pages. Citations may occupy
  unlimited additional pages. Rebuttal/camera-ready expands the limit to 10
  pages.
- Use US Letter paper, 10-point Times-preferred text, and the official margins
  and spacing. Do not modify the style file or override its dimensions/fonts.
- Use `natbib` author-year citations (`\citet` and `\citep`) and list
  references alphabetically. The official bibliography style is used here.
- Figure captions appear below figures; table captions appear above tables.
  Both must remain legible, centered/clean, and associated with their floats.
- Color is allowed, but captions and body text should remain meaningful in
  black and white.
- Pages must be numbered. Submission line numbers are generated by the style.
- The AI use statement is REQUIRED, must be at most one page, and does not
  count toward the page limit.
- An ethics statement is RECOMMENDED, at most one page, and does not count
  toward the page limit.
- A reproducibility statement is RECOMMENDED, should be paragraph length, and
  does not count toward the page limit.
- Author contributions are optional. Acknowledgments belong at the end of the
  paper and must not disclose identity in an anonymous submission.
- The example places statements before references and the appendix after the
  bibliography.
