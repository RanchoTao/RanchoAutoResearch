# Draft 2 visual QA

The final delivered PDF was rendered at 130 DPI into 11 page images and every
page was inspected.

## Page-by-page result

| Pages | Content | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1--3 | Title, abstract, introduction, related work, setup | PASS |
| 4--7 | Main result table, Figures 1--4, core evidence | PASS |
| 8 | Limitations, conclusion, required statements | PASS |
| 9 | References and beginning of Appendix A | PASS |
| 10--11 | Appendices A--L | PASS |

No clipping, overlap, missing glyph, blank page, truncated caption, unreadable
table, or out-of-margin figure was observed. Figure labels and axes remain
legible at the review-page scale. The page-number sequence is continuous.

The most visible compression tradeoff is density: the setup on page 3 and the
appendix provenance paragraph on page 9 are compact. Both remain readable, and
the latter is outside the main-text budget. No layout change is scientifically
necessary.
